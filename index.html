How to order

Ordering is as easy as 1, 2, 3

No accounts and no online payment. Just pick your snacks and send us your details.

  1. Add your snacks

    Open any product and tap Add to order on the flavours and sizes you like.

  2. Send your order

    Open Your order, adjust quantities and add your name and contact details.

  3. We confirm it

    Your order lands straight in our inbox. We will reply to confirm availability and the next steps.
